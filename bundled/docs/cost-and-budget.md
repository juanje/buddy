# Cost and Budget

Buddy uses a paid AI service to think and respond. Every message costs a small amount (fractions of a cent for short exchanges, a few cents for longer ones). This cost is transparent and controllable.

## Where costs come from

1. **Your messages** — each time Buddy responds, the AI processes your message and generates a response. This is the visible cost.
2. **Background maintenance** — Buddy automatically maintains its memory: reflecting after sessions, consolidating knowledge periodically. These run in the background without any action from you. They can cost more than a short chat because they process large volumes of context.

If you see a monthly total that's higher than what your conversations alone would explain, it's likely due to background maintenance. This is normal and expected.

## What you see

Open **Settings** (gear icon or Cmd/Ctrl+,) → the **Usage** section shows:

- **This session:** what this conversation has cost so far.
- **This month:** total across all sessions and background work, against your budget.
- A progress bar showing how close you are to your budget.

## Monthly budget

- A default budget ($10/month) protects you from unexpected spending.
- At **80%**, you'll get a notification warning you're approaching the limit.
- At **100%**, chat is paused — Buddy explains what happened and you can raise the budget in Settings to continue immediately.
- You can change the budget anytime, or remove it entirely if you prefer no limit.
- The budget resets automatically at the start of each month.

## Controlling costs

- **Switch to a cheaper model** — faster models cost significantly less per message. You can switch in Settings anytime.
- **Raise or lower the budget** — find the level that's comfortable for your usage pattern.
- **Expect background costs as part of normal operation** — maintenance runs on a fixed schedule regardless of how much you chat. With more conversations and knowledge accumulated, consolidations may process more content and cost slightly more. This is the cost of having an assistant that genuinely remembers and organizes.
