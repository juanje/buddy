## Task lifecycle and knowledge spaces

Tasks now have a lifecycle beyond add-and-done. You can park items for later, tasks with future dates stay out of the way until they're relevant, and brain dumps get classified item by item instead of all-or-nothing. A new knowledge layer — workspaces — separates raw working materials from the distilled wiki.

**What you could see**

- **Parking tasks.** "Not now" or "maybe later" moves a task to @someday instead of removing it. Parked and future-dated items show as a count at the bottom of task lists, not as clutter.
- **Brain dumps classified individually.** Send a list of things and each one gets its own classification — some become tasks, others context, others projects. Previously the whole message was treated as one thing.
- **Areas of focus.** Ongoing responsibilities ("health", "the blog") are now distinguished from finite projects. They get their own space and can spawn projects without being projects themselves.
- **Workspaces.** `user/workspaces/` holds raw and in-progress materials for an area of focus. The wiki stays for distilled, interconnected knowledge. Content flows from workspace to wiki, not the other way.
- **The assistant speaks as itself.** "I captured this as a task" instead of "Buddy has captured this as a task."
- **Tasks.md is read-only to the assistant.** Direct file edits are blocked — all changes go through the tasks tool, which keeps format and metadata consistent.

**What was one small change away from going wrong**

- **Empty sessions produced a reflect log entry.** If you opened and closed Buddy without saying anything, the session-end process still ran and wrote a contentless log. It now skips cleanly.

**What changed underneath**

Tasks carry a `created` date for staleness tracking. The daily consolidation reviews work-in-progress by counting only next-marked items, not the full task list. The system prompt (`agents-base.md`) was condensed by 30% — identity and language rules that duplicated SOUL.md were removed, three routing tables merged into one. Workspaces get a boot migration that adds navigation to AGENTS.md, and the weekly consolidation flags workspace content that may be ready for wiki ingestion.

1296 unit tests, 492 BDD scenarios. Everything should behave the same or better.
