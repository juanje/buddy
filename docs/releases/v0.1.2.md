## Consolidation stops draining the budget

A deterministic failure in background maintenance retried forever, and every retry was a billed model call. No attacker involved, and no warning.

- Retries now back off, with a **ceiling of three failures**, after which that depth is abandoned and you are told.
- A depth that **did** complete is no longer discarded because a later one failed.
- The spend cap is checked **before each depth**, not only when the cascade starts.
