## First-open orientation

The first time you open Buddy each day, a short orientation card appears above the chat — deferred reminders (if any) and your next tasks — plus a quiet "where we left off" line generated by the assistant after the session boots.

**What you could see**

- **An orientation card on first open of the day.** It lists due deferred items and next tasks from `user/tasks.md` (up to three, one per area). Long task text truncates with an ellipsis instead of overflowing the card. Dismiss it with the X or "Dismiss" — it won't come back until tomorrow, and due deferred items are acknowledged in one step (no second banner to close).
- **"Where we left off" replaces the welcome greeting.** After the session is ready, Buddy silently asks the agent for a one-sentence recap in your language. It appears as a note above the chat, not as a message bubble, and disappears when you send your first message.
- **No recap on later opens the same day** — topic transitions and second launches skip both the card (if already dismissed) and the one-liner request.

**What changed underneath**

New `getOrientationData`, `dismissOrientation`, and `requestOneLiner` worker RPCs; `lastOrientationDate` in `~/.buddy/config.json`. Agent events are suppressed during the silent recap so the response does not stream into the transcript. Six orientation unit tests; progress CLI `units` command arity fix (>= 4).

1254 unit tests, 453 BDD scenarios. Nothing else should look or behave differently.
