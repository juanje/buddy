## Deadlines, colleagues and reminders

Three things Buddy got wrong in daily use. It treated a task's due date as the day the task starts, so a task due next week vanished from your list and stopped counting as your next action. It could find a colleague on Slack but could not read your conversation with them on a workspace that restricts channel listing. And a reminder could pop up as a system notification while the app showed nothing about it.

**What you could see**

- **A task due next week was missing from your list, and "next" marks went uncounted.** Buddy hid every task whose due date was after today, as though the date were when the task becomes doable. A due date is a deadline: the task is on your list today, it can be the next action for its project, and that project counts as active work. Once the date passes, the task is overdue. To put something off, park it.
- **Dashboards could not list all your next actions.** A next action with a future due date was labelled "scheduled" instead of "next". That label is gone, so filtering a dashboard by next actions shows all of them.
- **Buddy said a task was not your next action when it was.** Adding a task to a project with no next action makes it the next one, but Buddy only replied "Task added." and then told you otherwise. It now says which project or area the task became next for.
- **A false alarm about too much work in progress.** Buddy compared the total number of next actions across all your areas with the per-area limit. That number is gone from what it reads. The daily review's reminder now names the projects it counts, and explains what it counts.
- **"Summarize my last conversation with …" on Slack ended with Buddy asking for a link.** It could look the person up, but it needed the channel list to find your direct messages, and corporate workspaces often block that. It now opens the conversation directly from the person, reads the most recent messages however old they are, and remembers the conversation for next time. Finding someone by name checks the people it already knows before asking Slack.
- **A reminder notification with nothing to show in the app.** After the morning summary card, reminders that came due later in the day fired a system notification, but the reminder panel stayed hidden for the rest of the session, and closing the panel kept it closed. Every reminder that arrives now reopens the panel with its text.

**What was one small change away from going wrong**

- **Refreshing the Slack channel list forgot the people Buddy had learned.** It replaced the saved list instead of adding to it, so the direct messages Buddy had recorded could disappear. It now adds to the list and keeps what it already knew.

**What changed underneath**

Several tests had pinned the old behaviour as though it were a requirement (future tasks hidden, the reminder panel suppressed for the session), and one could never fail. They now describe the new decisions and fail when the old behaviour comes back. A new check makes sure the instructions Buddy follows only mention task options and figures that actually exist, and the help page about active work now defines it the way Buddy counts it.

1,695 unit tests and 794 BDD scenarios pass. If a system notification from Buddy shows only its icon, check that "Show previews" is not set to "Never" in your notification settings.
