## Deferred notifications after orientation

Due reminders and decisions now continue to generate notifications after Buddy’s daily orientation card has been shown. Previously, closing the orientation card could prevent the same deferred items from appearing again in the app and from reaching the operating-system notification centre.

**What you could see**

- **Due deferred items stopped notifying after the orientation card appeared.** Buddy correctly detected them in the background, but incorrectly treated the orientation card as a reason to suppress all later deferred notifications.

**What changed underneath**

The orientation card still suppresses the duplicate in-app deferred banner during that session, while deferred items remain queued and system notifications continue normally. Added BDD and unit coverage for this interaction.

The full quality gate passes: 1,392 unit tests and 552 BDD scenarios. This is a hotfix; no other behaviour is intended to change.
