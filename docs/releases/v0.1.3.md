## Network boundary and permissions in maintenance

- **`fetch_url` can no longer reach your local network.** The URL is chosen by the agent, whose context is shaped by pages it has already read — attacker-influenced input, not an intention of yours. Blocked by hostname, by resolved address, and again on every redirect.
- Fetched content is framed explicitly **as data, not as instructions**.
- **The maintenance session ran with no permission layer at all.** It is autonomous and there is nobody to ask, so it now refuses everything outside the buddy directory and records the attempts.
