## Confined links, neutralized HTML

The two findings with a confirmed exploit path, from an external code review.

- **A link in the chat could take you outside the buddy directory.** Links written by the agent were resolved in the frontend, and `../../../` reached the disk. Containment now lives in the worker, which revalidates every request: the frontend decides what to *offer*, never what may be *read*.
- **Buddy opened files with external programs.** Clicking a `.command` file was execution, not reading. That capability was withdrawn entirely — only `.md` and `.txt` open, inside the app.
- **Raw HTML in the agent's replies was rendered.** It is now escaped, and a restrictive CSP blocks loading anything external.
- The About text no longer promises more than it delivers: what stays on your machine is your files and your memory, not the conversation.
