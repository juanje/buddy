## Close cards without losing reminders

Closing the morning orientation card no longer treats your reminders as done. The deferred banner now has two actions, so you can clear the chat and still be reminded later — or acknowledge items when you have actually seen them. Wrap-up also asks whether to capture pending items as tasks, and Buddy's own help pages now describe how tasks, projects, and routing actually work.

**What you could see**

- **Closing the first-open card deleted the reminders it showed.** You wanted to get the card out of the way; the due items vanished from the queue and never came back. Closing it now only hides the card for the rest of the day. The reminders stay, and they do not immediately jump in as a second card in the same session.
- **The reminder banner had only "Got it".** There was no way to hide it without acknowledging. It now has **Close** (hide for now — items return on the next check or the next session) and **Got it** (you have seen them; they leave the queue).
- **Wrap-up listed pending items and a next action, then stopped.** You had to ask Buddy to turn those into tasks. After a wrap-up, if it found pending items or a concrete next step, it now asks whether to capture them — and only adds them if you agree.
- **Asking Buddy what it can do still described an older task model.** One next action per area, warnings when adding tasks, little about projects, parking, or starting the day. The built-in pages now match current behaviour: next action per project, capture never blocked, areas of focus, wrap-up, and how most of what you share is treated as context unless you ask otherwise.

**What changed underneath**

The bundled self-documentation was brought in line with the task and routing model shipped since v0.1.35. Wrap-up is still a prompt change in the existing closure turn — no new buttons.
