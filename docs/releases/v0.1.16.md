## Buddy learns what you like

Buddy now accepts more file formats — CSV, JSON, YAML, and log files — and
gives actionable guidance when you drop an unsupported format: spreadsheets
get "export to CSV" advice, documents get "export to plain text". Under the
hood, consolidation gained a new step that reviews each day's conversations
for preference signals and personal facts, keeping your profile current
without you having to ask.

**What you could see**

- **Dropping a .csv, .json, .yaml, or .log file now works.** Previously only
  .md and .txt were accepted. The new formats are ingested as plain text.

- **Unsupported files get specific advice instead of a generic error.** A
  .xlsx gets "export to CSV from your spreadsheet app"; a .docx gets "export
  to plain text". Files with no extension are now rejected with guidance
  instead of silently ignored.

- **Your preferences are tracked across sessions.** Consolidation now reviews
  the day's interactions for preference changes, new personal facts, and
  corrections — and updates your profile accordingly. You don't need to
  repeat yourself.

**What changed underneath**

Existing USER.md files without a Preferences section gain one automatically
at session boot — a deterministic migration, not a model rewrite. The
consolidation prompt includes size discipline for USER.md (under 60 lines,
with satellite files for detail), keeping the identity payload fast to load.
The process-conversation skill now detects preference-change signals
alongside its existing observation types.
